# Governance

Angola Legal is an open project maintained through public, documented contributions. This initial governance model is intentionally lightweight.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose code, documentation, taxonomy, or metadata.
- **Maintainers** review changes, protect repository integrity, and may merge or decline contributions.
- **Subject reviewers** may provide expertise without implying official endorsement.

No participant speaks for an Angolan public authority unless formally authorized to do so. Participation does not create legal authority.

## Decisions

Use issues and pull requests for substantive changes. Seek consensus, document unresolved trade-offs, and provide reasons for decisions. Maintainers may make routine editorial and operational decisions. Changes to rights policy, core schema, scope, or governance should receive public discussion.

## Stewardship

Maintain provenance, version history, and transparent correction records. Prefer reversible changes. When a rights, privacy, or accuracy concern is substantiated, restrict or remove affected material promptly while documenting the action without exposing sensitive data.

## Maintainers

The repository owner may appoint or remove maintainers and should document role changes in repository history. This document may evolve as the community grows.
