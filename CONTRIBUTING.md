# Contributing | Contribuir

Thank you for helping build a traceable catalogue of legal knowledge related to Angola.

## Before contributing

Read the editorial policy, data license, rights and privacy guidance, and the relevant schema. Never submit real legal text or third-party content unless the right to publish it has been checked. A URL and metadata are often sufficient.

## Records

- Use the JSON Schema in `schemas/legal-document.schema.json`.
- Keep document nature, legal force, temporal status, verification status, and reuse rights distinct.
- Cite source URLs and record access dates when available.
- Mark uncertain, inferred, and unknown values explicitly; do not fill gaps with guesses.
- Do not claim a record is current merely because it appears on a website.
- Use a stable project identifier; preserve official identifiers separately.
- The example record is fictitious and must not be cited as law.

## Changes

Submit focused changes with a clear description, evidence, and references. For taxonomy or editorial changes, explain the effect on existing records. For code changes, describe expected behavior and run the available validator or workflow checks.

## Review

Maintainers may request evidence, rights clarification, privacy minimization, or changes to uncertainty labels. Contributions can be declined where provenance or lawful publication cannot be established. See `GOVERNANCE.md` for decision-making.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful, precise, and open to correction. Critique evidence and proposals, not people. Harassment, intimidation, and discriminatory conduct are not acceptable.
