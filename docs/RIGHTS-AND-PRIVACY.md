# Rights and privacy guidance

## Rights

Before publishing source content, identify the rights holder or applicable legal basis and the permitted acts. Separate access from copying, redistribution, translation, adaptation, and commercial use. If permission is unclear, publish a citation or link only where lawful and set reuse status to `unknown` or `link_only`.

Keep evidence of licenses or permissions and their scope. Do not imply that the repository owns third-party materials. The project’s MIT code license and metadata contribution terms do not license source documents.

## Privacy

Collect only personal data necessary for a clearly stated legal, historical, or research purpose. Public records may still contain sensitive or identifying information. Consider redaction, minimization, aggregation, or link-only treatment. Do not publish private addresses, contact details, identifiers, credentials, or sensitive data merely because they appear in a source.

## Review and requests

Flag uncertain rights or privacy issues before publication. Provide the affected record ID or path and a concise explanation; avoid reproducing sensitive information in public issues. Maintainers should assess promptly, limit access or remove disputed material where warranted, preserve an appropriate audit note, and seek clarification where needed.

This guidance is operational project policy, not legal advice.
