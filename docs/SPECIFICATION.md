# Project specification | Especificação do projecto

## Purpose

Angola Legal is an open, traceable catalogue of legal knowledge related to Angola. The project covers discoverable legal materials and their relationships; it does not assert that it is an official source or a complete corpus.

## In scope

Metadata, identifiers, provenance, links, status, relationships, version history, and where lawful, document representations for legal and legally relevant materials. The scope includes legislation, regulations, judicial decisions, administrative materials, international instruments, legislative history, procurement and public contracts, collective and professional rules, doctrine, research, forms, notices, and historical records.

## Out of scope for the initial release

- Claims that all Angolan legal materials are present or current.
- Unauthorized copying or redistribution of source texts.
- Legal advice, authoritative interpretation, or adjudication.
- Inference of legal effect from document type, publication, or web availability alone.

## Required distinctions

A record keeps separate fields for document nature, legal force, temporal status, verification status, and reuse rights. Each answers a different question:

- **Nature:** what kind of item is it?
- **Legal force:** what effect, if any, does it have?
- **Temporal status:** what is its relationship to time and amendments?
- **Verification:** how well have identity and assertions been checked?
- **Reuse rights:** what may this project publish or redistribute?

Unknown is a legitimate value. Metadata changes should retain evidence and provenance.

## Initial deliverables

Bilingual project documentation; taxonomy; architecture proposal; editorial, rights, and privacy policies; JSON Schema; clearly fictitious example; Python validator; automated validation workflow; contribution and governance guidance.

## Acceptance principles

Records should be traceable to sources; rights should be explicit; uncertainty must remain visible; relationships and historical changes should not erase prior states. See the schema and editorial policy for operational details.
