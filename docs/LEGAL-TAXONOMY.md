# Legal materials taxonomy | Taxonomia de materiais jurídicos

This open taxonomy classifies legally relevant items related to Angola. It is intentionally broader than legislation and is not an assertion that every class is populated.

## Primary normative materials

- Constitution and constitutional amendments or instruments.
- Laws, codes, statutes, legislative decrees, presidential decrees, executive decrees, resolutions, orders, regulations, instructions, and other normative acts.
- Implementing measures, amendments, corrigenda, repeals, consolidations, and historical versions.
- Sectoral rules and technical or professional standards with legal relevance.

## Courts and dispute resolution

- Constitutional, supreme, appellate, provincial, and other judicial decisions where lawfully publishable.
- Administrative and arbitral decisions, opinions, procedural rules, and published summaries.
- Case identifiers, cited provisions, and related decisions.

## Public administration and regulatory practice

- Administrative decisions, circulars, directives, notices, guidance, forms, fee schedules, public registers, and official manuals.
- Regulatory decisions, licenses, sanctions, and published compliance materials, subject to privacy and access checks.

## International and regional law

- Treaties, conventions, protocols, declarations, accession and ratification instruments, reservations, and implementing measures.
- Regional and international decisions or materials relevant to Angola.

## Law-making process

- Bills, proposals, explanatory memoranda, committee reports, opinions, consultation papers, parliamentary debates, votes, and other preparatory works.

## Public finance, procurement, and public agreements

- Budgets, public accounts, procurement notices and awards, publishable contracts, concessions, public-private partnership instruments, and audit reports.
- Record confidentiality and personal data constraints separately.

## Employment, professional, and institutional rules

- Collective agreements, employment rules, professional statutes, ethics codes, disciplinary rules, institutional bylaws, and public-service instruments.

## Legal knowledge and practice

- Legal doctrine, commentaries, academic articles, theses, research, reports, and bibliographies.
- Court forms, procedural guides, legal aid materials, manuals, and practical tools.
- Clearly identify non-binding nature and third-party rights.

## Historical and evidentiary materials

- Repealed or superseded materials, official gazettes and indexes, archival catalogues, legislative history, and records useful for tracing legal development.

## Classification

Use the closest broad `document_nature` in the schema and explain more specific types in `document_nature_detail`. Subject matter is a separate, multi-valued descriptor. A document may be legally relevant without being binding law.
